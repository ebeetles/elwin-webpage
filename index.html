PREDICT

A benchmark for LLM-generated Alzheimer's drug-repurposing hypotheses. When an LLM judging novelty got falsified across four experiments, I replaced it with a structural PMI metric on Swanson's ABC model, calibrated per-drug against empirical nulls. Paper in progress, targeting ICLR.

07
Creative · Rhythm Game

Crossy Beat

A rhythm-game remix of Crossy Road: hop across traffic, water, and trains on the beat, or lose your run. Levels are procedurally generated and solvability-checked with a BFS so every batch is beatable, and a Tone.js clock stays the single source of truth for timing — kept strictly separate from the Three.js renderer, which only interpolates between beats.
